# Product

## What it is
Seed2Store is a direct marketplace for agricultural lots. Growers register a harvest lot (crop, variety, origin, harvest date, grade, moisture, photos), receive a tamper-evident certificate of origin (keccak-256 fingerprint, optionally minted as an ERC-721), and sell it through buy-now, private offers or timed auctions. Buyers verify provenance before committing and track orders through payment, shipping and delivery.

## Users
- **Growers** (farmers, cooperatives, estates): list lots, run auctions, answer offers, confirm payment and ship. Often on mobile, outdoors or in a warehouse.
- **Buyers** (mills, roasters, processors, exporters): browse and filter lots, bid, negotiate and track orders. Usually at a desk.
- **Anyone downstream**: verifies a certificate by serial, token ID or hash, without an account.

## Principles
- Provenance is the product. The certificate is the hero object, everywhere.
- Never fake chain activity. Off-chain records are labelled as content-hashed; on-chain ones show real token IDs and tx hashes.
- Works with zero configuration; every external service is an upgrade, never a requirement.
- Prices in USD for humans; ETH only at settlement.
